# Storyboard v1: Matsense Reels for 30+ belts
Narration in English (Meitar, 2026-10-10). Footage: any match in the Matsense app (Meitar, 2026-10-10).
The edit is cut on the narrator's recording, so all timings get fixed once it arrives.

## Material in hand
- **Match B** "Meitar Vs Amit" (gi, IBJJF, landscape 1280x720, 10:44). Analysis has three sweeps:
  4:24 (B), 5:58 (A), 6:52 (B); a guard pull at 0:23. Good for 9:16 crops: the athletes fill about 40% of the height.
- **Match A** "Meitar vs Amit" (no-gi, vertical phone, 11:25). Analysis: dangerous passing position 1:42, leg
  submission attempt 4:15, sweep 4:22, guard pass 5:35, foot-lock attempt 7:40, sweep attempt 8:58,
  referee stand-ups. Wide shot, small athletes: used as "your phone video" and inside the phone.
- **App screens** captured at iPhone size from staging (home and streak ring, match timeline, Fight
  breakdown, Progress, Library). The staging data is test data (no strong areas, "Athletes not told
  apart"), so the screens in the ads are rebuilt in code from the Glow design system and filled with
  these matches' real events.

## Look
- Real phone and competition footage, graded slightly darker and warmer, with light grain. It is
  never made to look generated (no AI-made grappling: it melts bodies).
- The app as the hero object: the Glow night UI (Manrope, the streak flame gradient, event
  colours) on a phone and full-bleed, moving with the voice.
- Captions: big word-by-word captions in the centre-safe area, timed to the narrator's words. The
  hook line is the largest type in each ad.
- Sound: the narrator in front, a low pulse bed under them (ducked), a crowd and mat-slap
  room tone on match shots, and a UI tick on every event marker.
- The end card in every ad: the Matsense mark, "matsense.co", 1.5 s.

## Ad 1: "Which advantage?"
0-2 s hook: a freeze on a scoreboard 2-2, advantages 0-1 in red; text LOST BY ONE ADVANTAGE.
Then match B plays fast and unreadable (the blur of the match) -> "Matsense watches it for you":
the phone, and the timeline fills marker by marker on the real event times -> one marker turns
red and opens ("Including the one that cost you") -> Tap / Watch / Fix: three hard cuts on the
voice -> end card.

## Ad 2: "Twice a day"
0-2 s hook: split screen, a 22-year-old's week (14 sessions lit up) vs yours (2 lit, the
Week strip from the app). Then a clock at 21:40 and a kitchen-light grade on the match video ->
"out-think them": the Fight breakdown switches Strong / Weak / Improve -> an area becomes a
task -> the plan sheet fills the week -> "Zero wasted mat time": the streak flame lights -> end card.

## Ad 3: "Forty students"
0-2 s hook: a grid of 40 match thumbnails, one is yours, and a stopwatch reads 1:30.
"That's the math": 40 x 6 min = 4 hours, written as numbers -> Matsense plays all six
minutes of match B at speed with the exchanges ticking onto the timeline -> the breakdown in
words -> a coach and student over the phone, shot as an over-the-shoulder still from the
match footage of the mat edge -> end card.

## Ad 4: "Forty minutes of staring"
0-2 s hook: a thumb scrubbing match A back and forth, the scrub bar jittering, the timer reading
00:00 -> 40:00. "Scrub. Wait. Scrub." on three cuts. Then "Matsense skips the staring": the dead
time collapses (the match strip folds into only its coloured events) -> one tap jumps to the
sweep at 4:22 -> "Your lunch break is enough": a 12:47 clock -> end card.

## Ad 5: "Same pass. Three tournaments."
0-2 s hook: three vertical panels side by side, three passing moments playing in sync, all taken from the two matches in the app (Meitar: work with what is there) (matches
A and B at their pass and dangerous-position moments, plus one more cut), each stamped with a
tournament name and date. "That's a pattern": the three panels slide into one Library folder ->
"Turn the pattern into a task": Train -> a task card -> the next-competition countdown tile
("21 days") -> "Then let them try that pass again": back to the panel, now held -> end card.

## Rights
- Footage: Meitar's own matches, used with his OK. The opponent appears in it.
- Marks: the IBJJF logo is visible on match B's banners. It is incidental, but it can be cropped out.

## Waiting on
