# Matsense Reels: 30+ hobbyist belts, narration scripts v1
slug: matsense-reels-30plus · created: 2026-10-10 · status: script, waiting on the narrator's recording

## Brief
- **Product:** Matsense (matsense.co). Upload a BJJ match video. It marks every moment that
  happened (points, advantages, near-misses, for both athletes) and writes a Fight Breakdown
  (strong areas, weak areas, areas to improve). It turns an area into a training task and plans
  the week up to the next competition.
- **Audience:** white, blue and purple belts aged 30+. They have jobs and families, train 2-4 times
  a week and compete in masters divisions. They film their matches but don't know what happened
  in them, and their coach has no time to go through every student's match.
- **Destination:** Instagram Reels / TikTok, 9:16, 20-28 s, burned-in captions (most people watch muted).
- **Hook rule:** the first 2 s hold a line that hurts, on screen and in the voice-over at the same time.
- **Narration language:** English (the app is in English). A native Hebrew version can be written if wanted.
- **Pace:** ~140 words a minute, so each script is 50-65 words.
- **The one feeling:** "I'm not too old or too busy for this. I've been training blind."

## Ad 1: "Which advantage?"
Hook on screen: **LOST BY ONE ADVANTAGE.** A scoreboard frozen at 2-2, advantages 0-1.

> You lost by one advantage.
> Which one?
> ...You don't know. Nobody remembers their own match. Adrenaline wipes it.
> Matsense watches it for you. Every pass, every sweep, every almost. Marked on the timeline.
> Including the one that cost you.
> Tap it. Watch it. Fix it.
> Matsense. Know what happened.

## Ad 2: "Twice a day"
Hook on screen: **THEY TRAIN TWICE A DAY. YOU TRAIN TWICE A WEEK.**

> The twenty-two-year-olds train twice a day.
> You train twice a week. After work. If the kids are asleep.
> You can't out-train them. So out-think them.
> Upload your match. Matsense breaks it down: what's working, what's leaking, what to drill this week.
> Then it builds your week around it.
> Less mat time. Zero wasted mat time.
> Matsense.

## Ad 3: "Forty students"
Hook on screen: **YOUR COACH HAS 40 STUDENTS. AND ONE PAIR OF EYES.**

> Your coach has forty students. One pair of eyes.
> Your match got about ninety seconds of them.
> Not their fault. That's the math.
> Matsense watches all six minutes. Every exchange, timed. A breakdown in plain words:
> your strong areas, your weak areas, what to fix next.
> Bring it to your next private. Watch how different that hour feels.
> Matsense.

## Ad 4: "Forty minutes of staring"
Hook on screen: **THIS IS HOW YOU WATCH YOUR MATCHES.** A thumb dragging the scrub bar back and forth.

> This is how you watch your own match.
> Scrub. Wait. Scrub.
> Six minutes of fight. Forty minutes of staring.
> And you still don't know where it turned.
> Matsense skips the staring. It marks every moment that mattered, yours and your opponent's.
> One tap, and you're there.
> Your lunch break is enough now.
> Matsense.

## Ad 5: "Same pass. Three tournaments."
Hook on screen: **SAME PASS. THREE TOURNAMENTS.** Three clips side by side, the same pass happening in each.

> Same pass. Three tournaments.
> That's not bad luck. That's a pattern.
> Patterns hide across months of matches. Matsense finds them.
> Every match goes into your library, and the same moments are filed together.
> Turn the pattern into a task. Train it. Count down to your next competition.
> Then let them try that pass again.
> Matsense.

## Recording notes for the narrator
- Voice: calm, close to the mic, like a training partner who has seen it all. Not an announcer and not hype.
  The hook line is quiet and direct, and the "Matsense" at the end is short.
- Record each ad as its own file, each line twice, with a 1 s silence between lines.
  The edit picks the best take of each line.
- "..." means a real pause (about 1 s). A line break means a short breath.
- A quiet room and a phone held 20 cm from the mouth is enough. Send WAV or the phone's
  original voice-memo file (not a WhatsApp voice note, which is compressed).
