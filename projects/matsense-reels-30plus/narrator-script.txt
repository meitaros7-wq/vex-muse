MATSENSE - 5 REELS ADS - NARRATION SCRIPT

HOW TO RECORD
- Calm, close to the mic, like a training partner who has seen it all. Not an announcer, not hype.
- The first line of each ad is the hook: quiet, direct, it should sting a little.
- One file per ad. Read each line twice, with a 1-second pause between lines.
- "..." means a real pause (about 1 second).
- Quiet room, phone about 20 cm from the mouth. Send the original voice-memo file, not a WhatsApp voice note.


AD 1 - "Which advantage?"

You lost by one advantage.
Which one?
...You don't know. Nobody remembers their own match. Adrenaline wipes it.
Matsense watches it for you. Every pass, every sweep, every almost. Marked on the timeline.
Including the one that cost you.
Tap it. Watch it. Fix it.
Matsense. Know what happened.


AD 2 - "Twice a day"

The twenty-two-year-olds train twice a day.
You train twice a week. After work. If the kids are asleep.
You can't out-train them. So out-think them.
Upload your match. Matsense breaks it down: what's working, what's leaking, what to drill this week.
Then it builds your week around it.
Less mat time. Zero wasted mat time.
Matsense.


AD 3 - "Forty students"

Your coach has forty students. One pair of eyes.
Your match got about ninety seconds of them.
Not their fault. That's the math.
Matsense watches all six minutes. Every exchange, timed. A breakdown in plain words:
your strong areas, your weak areas, what to fix next.
Bring it to your next private. Watch how different that hour feels.
Matsense.


AD 4 - "Forty minutes of staring"

This is how you watch your own match.
Scrub. Wait. Scrub.
Six minutes of fight. Forty minutes of staring.
And you still don't know where it turned.
Matsense skips the staring. It marks every moment that mattered, yours and your opponent's.
One tap, and you're there.
Your lunch break is enough now.
Matsense.


AD 5 - "Same pass. Three tournaments."

Same pass. Three tournaments.
That's not bad luck. That's a pattern.
Patterns hide across months of matches. Matsense finds them.
Every match goes into your library, and the same moments are filed together.
Turn the pattern into a task. Train it. Count down to your next competition.
Then let them try that pass again.
Matsense.
